I am a Researcher at the [ETH AI Center](https://ai.ethz.ch), where I work on post-training of LLMs for the [Swiss AI Initiative](https://www.swiss-ai.org), contributing to [Apertus](https://www.swiss-ai.org/apertus). Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher (and Young Investigator) at the [Allen Institute for AI](https://allenai.org) and the University of Washington, advised by Prof. Hanna Hajishirzi and Prof. Yejin Choi. There I worked on open post-training efforts such as [Tülu 3](https://allenai.org/papers/tulu-3-report.pdf) and contributed to [OLMo](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.00838), work recognized with the ACL [Best Theme Paper Award](http://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.00838).

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at the [NLP lab of Bar Ilan University](https://biu-nlp.github.io), supervised by Prof. Ido Dagan and Prof. Reut Tsarfaty, during which I was fortunate to spend time as a visiting student at [UW NLP](https://nlp.washington.edu) with Prof. Yejin Choi and to intern twice at the [Allen Institute for AI](https://allenai.org), where I received the [Outstanding Intern of the Year Award](https://allenai.org/outstanding-interns). Earlier, I interned at Google Research, obtained an MSc in Artificial Intelligence with distinction from the University of Edinburgh, and a BA from the University of Zurich.

My research interests center on post-training and adaptation of language models — making them precisely steerable. I care about how models follow instructions and constraints, and how preference and reward signals shape their behavior. This has led me to build benchmarks like [IFBench](https://github.com/allenai/IFBench) and [RewardBench](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.13787), and to work broadly across data quality, evaluation methodology, and the science of LLM development. My work has been awarded an [ACL Outstanding Paper Award](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.16786) and featured in the press, for example by [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/21/ai2s-open-source-tulu-3-lets-anyone-play-the-ai-post-training-game/?guccounter=1) and [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2024/ai2s-new-tulu-3-model-rivals-tech-giants-in-breakthrough-for-open-source-ai-post-training/).

## Research
{: #research .section-heading}

My research focuses on how one can develop generative AI that is contextually robust, responsible and open. In particular, I have focused on extending language models' capabilities through post-training and adaptation. Additionally, I have been involved in the construction of multiple, widely-used benchmarks, such as RewardBench! More specifically, my research is centered around:

- **Open Science of LLMs and Post-Training:** Developing good open recipes for language model post-training.
  - I am a core contributor on the Tulu and [Open-Instruct](https://github.com/allenai/open-instruct) project, where we develop post-training pipelines consisting of supervised finetuning, direct preference optimization, and reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards.
  - I have worked on the open science of language models, by contributing to [OLMo](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.00838.pdf) and [OLMo 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.00656).
- **Steerability, Underspecification and Context:** Improving how models deal with contextual robustness, underspecified inputs, and how they can respond more precisely to instructions.
- **Critical Evaluation:** Building challenging benchmarks for more realistic, human-centered evaluation of generative models and reward models.
